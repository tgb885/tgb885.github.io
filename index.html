About

I am a B.Sc. student in Computer Science & Engineering at United International University (UIU), Dhaka, Bangladesh. I took heavy CSE core courses early, including Data Structures & Algorithms, Discrete Mathematics and Digital Logic Design.

Research Statement

My interest in research comes from building things that did not work the first time. Working on Amnic showed me how little I knew about why models fail on low-resource languages and limited hardware. How to make AI work under real constraints is the question I want to study properly.

In parallel, I am designing an object-tracking drone, from its frame to its control code. To guide the design, I read and summarise papers on quadrotor aerodynamics and keep a structured note for each one. This habit taught me how research is built step by step, and it made me want to work inside a lab rather than only read about one.

My academic record is still developing: my CGPA rose from 2.22 in my first trimester to 3.00 now. I am looking for a research internship or summer school where I can learn how real research questions are framed, tested and written up. I can contribute steady programming in C/C++ and Python, careful literature reading, and the persistence of someone who has built projects without a big team or budget.

After that experience, I plan to bring what I learn back into my degree and my projects, and to prepare for graduate study in AI in Europe.
